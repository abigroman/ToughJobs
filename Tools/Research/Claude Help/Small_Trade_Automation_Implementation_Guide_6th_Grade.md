## AUTOMATION #1: MISSED-CALL TEXT-BACK
### (The One That Pays for Everything in 30 Days)

**What it does:**
When someone calls your business and you don't answer, the computer sends them a text right away. Instead of them calling your competitor, they text you back.

**Why you need this:**
- Right now, you miss about 7 out of every 10 calls
- 85% of people who can't reach you will call someone else instead
- If you're a plumber at $400 per call, those missed calls cost you $2,800 per week

**How much it costs:**
- Setup: $500–$1,500 (one-time payment to someone who knows how to do this)
- Per month: $49–$97 (for the software)
- Total first month: $549–$1,597

**How much you make back:**
- If you recover just 2 calls per week: $3,200 per month in new jobs
- You pay for the setup in less than one week
- You make pure profit after that

---

### HOW TO SET IT UP (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1: Pick Your Tool**
You need software that does this. The easiest ones for small businesses are:
- GoHighLevel ($97/month to start)
- Housecall Pro ($49–$189/month if you're HVAC)
- BuildFolio ($39/month)

Pick one. Sign up. They all have a free trial.

**Step 2: Connect Your Business Phone Number**
- Log into your new software
- Go to "Settings" or "Phone"
- Plug in your business phone number (the one customers call)
- The software now watches that number

**Step 3: Write Your Text Message**
This is what the customer gets. Keep it short. Here's an example:

*"Hey! Sorry we missed your call. What can we help with? Reply here and we'll get back to you ASAP. —[Your Name]'s Plumbing"*

Or for HVAC:

*"Thanks for calling! We missed you but we're here to help. Text back what you need and we'll book you in. —[Your Company]"*

**Step 4: Set Up the Trigger**
Tell the software: "When someone calls and we don't answer within 20 seconds, send that text."

Most software makes this super easy. Click a few buttons. That's it.

**Step 5: Test It**
- Call your business number from your personal phone
- Don't answer it
- Hang up
- You should get the text within 30 seconds
- If you do, it's working
- If you don't, call the software company's help line

**Step 6: Turn It On for Real**
Now it runs 24/7. Every missed call gets a text.

---

### WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

**The Customer's Side:**
1. They call you → call goes unanswered
2. They get a text 30 seconds later
3. They text back what they need
4. You see it in your software
5. You text back: "We can come Thursday at 2pm. Does that work?"
6. They say yes
7. You have a new job booked

**Your Side:**
1. You get a notification on your phone when they text back
2. You reply from the same software (or your phone)
3. It's all saved in one place
4. No more "where did that message go?"

---

### REAL EXAMPLE

**Before (Without Text-Back):**
- Phone rings while you're under a sink
- You can't answer
- Customer gets voicemail
- Customer never checks voicemail
- Customer calls the other plumber down the street
- Job is gone
- You never knew they called
- You just lost $400

**After (With Text-Back):**
- Phone rings while you're under a sink
- You can't answer
- Customer gets a text saying "Sorry we missed you, reply here"
- Customer sees the text 2 minutes later
- Customer texts: "I need my shower fixed asap"
- You see it on your phone (alerts you)
- You text back: "Can you do Thursday 2pm?"
- Customer says yes
- You get the job
- You made $400
- Total time you spent: 2 minutes

## AUTOMATION #2: APPOINTMENT REMINDERS
### (Stops No-Shows Dead)

**What it does:**
When a customer books an appointment, the software sends them a text 24 hours before reminding them. They show up. You don't waste time driving to empty houses.

**Why you need this:**
- Right now, about 15–20% of customers don't show up
- If you're a plumber, that's 2–3 jobs per week where you drove for nothing
- A wasted hour = $200–$400 lost

**How much it costs:**
- Setup: $200–$700
- Per month: Included in most CRM software (GoHighLevel, Housecall Pro, BuildFolio)
- Total first month: $200–$800

**How much you make back:**
- If you prevent just 2 no-shows per month, you save $800–$1,200
- You pay for the setup in one month

---

### HOW TO SET IT UP (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1: Use Your Scheduling Software**
When someone books an appointment, it needs to go into a system. Use:
- Google Calendar (free, simple)
- Calendly (free version, or $10/month)
- Your CRM software's built-in calendar

**Step 2: Turn On Automatic Reminders**
In the software settings, find "Reminders" or "Notifications"

Check the box that says: "Send reminder 24 hours before"

**Step 3: Write Your Reminder Text**
Keep it simple:

*"Hi Sarah! Just a reminder — we're coming to your house tomorrow (Wednesday) at 2:00 PM to fix your AC. Looking forward to seeing you!"*

Or shorter:

*"Appointment reminder: AC repair tomorrow at 2pm. Reply YES to confirm. —Tom's HVAC"*

**Step 4: Pick How to Send It**
You have options:
- Text message (fastest, 98% of people read it)
- Email (slower, but still works)
- Both (text AND email to be safe)

Pick text. Text is better.

**Step 5: Test It**
Book a fake appointment for tomorrow. Get the reminder. Make sure it works.

**Step 6: Turn It On**
Now every appointment gets a reminder text, automatically.

---

### WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

**The Customer's Side:**
1. They book an appointment (online, on the phone, or through your app)
2. The next day, they get a text: "Coming tomorrow at 2pm"
3. They see it and remember
4. They are home when you arrive
5. You do the job
6. Everyone is happy

**Your Side:**
1. When they text back "YES" to confirm, you see it
2. No surprises
3. No wasted trips
4. More jobs done per day
5. More money in your pocket

---

### REAL EXAMPLE

**Before (Without Reminders):**
- Customer books an appointment for Thursday at 2pm
- You put it in your calendar
- Wednesday morning, they forget
- You drive to their house
- Nobody is home
- You drive back
- You just wasted an hour
- That hour was worth $300–$400
- You're now mad
- You have an empty slot and nothing to do

**After (With Reminders):**
- Customer books an appointment for Thursday at 2pm
- Software sends a reminder text Wednesday at 2pm
- Customer sees it and remembers
- Customer is home Thursday at 2pm
- You show up and do the job
- You made $400–$600
- No wasted time
- Next appointment is easy to get to because you saved the hour

## AUTOMATION #3: REVIEW REQUESTS
### (Your Customers Sell for You)

**What it does:**
After you finish a job, the software automatically sends the customer a text asking them to leave a review on Google. They say yes. Your reviews go up. More people call you because they see good reviews.

**Why you need this:**
- Customers with 20+ reviews on Google get 40% more calls than people with 0 reviews
- Right now, you probably ask for reviews sometimes and forget other times
- With automation, you ask every single customer the same way, every single time

**How much it costs:**
- Setup: $300–$1,000
- Per month: Usually free if you already have CRM software
- Total first month: $300–$1,000

**How much you make back:**
- More reviews = more people calling you
- If you get 5 extra calls per month from better reviews: $2,000–$3,000 in new jobs
- You pay for the setup in less than one month

---

### HOW TO SET IT UP (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1: Set Up Your Google Business Profile**
(This is free. Customers see it when they Google your business name.)

- Go to google.com/business
- Search for your business
- Click "Claim this business"
- Follow the steps (takes 10 minutes)
- Verify your email
- Now you have a Google Business page where people can leave reviews

**Step 2: Get Your Google Review Link**
Once your profile is set up:
- In Google Business Profile, click "Customers"
- Click "Reviews"
- You'll see a link that says "Share" or "Get link"
- Copy that link
- Save it somewhere you can find it

**Step 3: Pick Your CRM Software**
Use GoHighLevel, Housecall Pro, BuildFolio, or similar

**Step 4: Create Your Review Request Message**
Write a simple text that asks for a review. Here are examples:

*"Hey Tom! Thanks so much for letting us fix your AC. Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? Your feedback really helps us! [link]"*

Or shorter:

*"Thanks for choosing us! Leave a review on Google: [your review link] Takes 30 seconds!"*

**Step 5: Set the Trigger**
Tell the software: "When a job is marked complete, send this review request text"

Most software does this in 2 clicks.

**Step 6: Test It**
Mark a job as complete. See if you get the review request text.

---

### WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

**The Customer's Side:**
1. You finish the job
2. They get a text: "Leave us a Google review please!"
3. They click the link
4. Google opens up
5. They write something like: "Great work! Showed up on time, cleaned up, very professional. 5 stars!"
6. Done

**Your Side:**
1. You get a notification that you have a new review
2. Their review shows up on Google
3. Next month, 10 more people see your good reviews
4. 2 of them call you
5. You get 2 new jobs
6. You make $800–$1,200
7. From one automation

---

### REAL EXAMPLE

**Before (Without Automation):**
- You finish fixing someone's furnace
- You intend to ask for a review, but you're tired
- You don't ask
- The customer is happy but doesn't know where to leave a review
- You end the day with 0 new reviews
- You ask some customers and forget others
- After 6 months, you have 5 reviews
- People see you have few reviews
- They call your competitor instead

**After (With Automation):**

## AUTOMATION #4: QUOTE/BID FOLLOW-UP
### (Bring Dead Deals Back to Life)

**What it does:**
After you send someone a quote, the software automatically sends them friendly follow-up texts on day 3, day 7, day 14, and day 30. Most people say yes the second or third time you ask.

**Why you need this:**
- 65% of people who get a quote from you are still deciding, but if you don't follow up, they forget
- They call your competitor instead
- You just left a $400–$5,000 job on the table
- Right now, you follow up on some quotes and forget others

**How much it costs:**
- Setup: $500–$2,000
- Per month: $97–$297 (software)
- Total first month: $597–$2,297

**How much you make back:**
- If you send 10 quotes per month and recover 2 of them (15% recovery), that's $800–$10,000 in new jobs
- You pay for the setup in one month or less

---

### HOW TO SET IT UP (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1: Use Your CRM**
You need to use software that tracks quotes. Use:
- GoHighLevel
- Housecall Pro
- BuildFolio
- Jobber

When you send a quote, it goes into this software (not just an email).

**Step 2: Create Your Follow-Up Messages**
Write 4 text messages. Here's an example for a plumber:

**Message 1 (Sent 3 days after quote):**
*"Hi Sarah! Just checking in on that bathroom quote we sent. Any questions? Happy to walk you through it. —Mike's Plumbing"*

**Message 2 (Sent 7 days after quote):**
*"Sarah, we're still holding a spot on our schedule for your bathroom. Let us know if you want to move forward!"*

**Message 3 (Sent 14 days after quote):**
*"Sarah, have you decided on the bathroom project? If you want to wait, that's okay — we'll be here. Just let us know!"*

**Message 4 (Sent 30 days after quote):**
*"Sarah, the bathroom quote expires in 3 days. If you want to lock in that price, reply YES or call us ASAP!"*

**Step 3: Set the Trigger**
In your CRM, go to "Automations" and set it up:

"When a quote is sent, wait 3 days, then send Message 1. Wait 4 more days, then send Message 2. And so on."

Most software has templates for this already.

**Step 4: Test It**
Send yourself a fake quote. Watch the texts come in on days 3, 7, 14, and 30. If they do, it's working.

**Step 5: Turn It On**
Now every quote you send gets automatically followed up on, without you thinking about it.

---

### WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

**The Customer's Side:**
1. They get a quote from you
2. Day 3: They get a friendly text checking in
3. They might reply "still thinking" or "when can you start?"
4. Day 7: Another text
5. Day 14: Another text
6. Day 30: Text that says "price expires soon"
7. They finally decide: "Yes, let's do it"
8. You book the job

**Your Side:**
1. You send the quote
2. You do nothing else
3. The software follows up for you, automatically
4. Some people say yes to the first text
5. Some say yes to the fourth text
6. Either way, the job is booked
7. You never had to think about it again

---

### REAL EXAMPLE

**Before (Without Follow-Up Automation):**
- You send a $3,000 bathroom remodel quote
- Customer says "let me think about it"
- You forget to follow up
- Customer thinks you forgot about them
- Customer calls the other contractor
- 2 weeks later: other contractor is doing the job for $2,800
- You just lost a $3,000 job because you forgot to text them twice

**After (With Follow-Up Automation):**
- You send a $3,000 bathroom remodel quote
- Software sends text on day 3: "Any questions on the quote?"
- Customer replies "Still thinking"
- Software sends text on day 7: "We're still here if you want to move forward"
- Customer sees second text
- They decide: "Yes, let's do it"
- You book the $3,000 job
- You make $600–$900 profit on it
- The automation paid for itself with one job

## AUTOMATION #5: SIMPLE CRM (Customer Relationship Manager)
### (Stop Losing Information in Your Brain)

**What it does:**
Every customer goes into one place. You can see: their name, their address, their phone, what you fixed for them, when you last talked to them, what they need next. You never lose a customer's information again. You never forget to call someone back.

**Why you need this:**
- Right now, customer info is probably scattered: some in your phone, some on business cards, some you just remember
- You forget to call people back
- You don't know what you fixed for them last time
- You can't easily figure out who to call about a recall or maintenance

**How much it costs:**
- Setup: $500–$1,500 (someone helps you set it up)
- Per month: $49–$297 (most software includes this)
- Total first month: $549–$1,797

**How much you make back:**
- Better follow-up = 10–15% more revenue from existing customers
- If you do $400,000 in revenue, that's $40,000–$60,000 per year extra
- You pay for the setup in the first two months

---

### HOW TO SET IT UP (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1: Pick Your CRM Software**
For small trade businesses, use:
- GoHighLevel ($97/month)
- Housecall Pro ($49–$189/month)
- BuildFolio ($39/month)
- Even Google Sheets with simple organization works

Pick one.

**Step 2: Create Your Customer Record**
Every time someone becomes a customer, create a record. In the software, you'll fill in:

**Basic Info:**
- First name: Sarah
- Last name: Johnson
- Phone: 555-0123
- Address: 123 Main St, Denver, CO
- Email: sarah@email.com

**Job Info:**
- What did you fix? "Water heater replacement"
- When? "March 15, 2024"
- Price: "$1,200"
- Next service due? "Maintenance in March 2025"

**Notes:**
- "Prefers texts over calls"
- "Has a dog (friendly)"
- "Wants to replace AC in summer"

**Step 3: Every Time You Finish a Job, Update It**
After you leave their house:
- Write down what you did
- Write down the date
- Write down what might need fixing next
- Mark when the next maintenance is due

Takes 2 minutes.

**Step 4: Set Your Phone to Check the CRM**
Before you go to a job:
- Look up their address in the CRM
- See their notes ("prefers texts," "has dog," etc.)
- Know what you fixed last time
- You show up prepared

Takes 1 minute.

**Step 5: Run Reports**
Once a month, the software can tell you:
- Who needs a maintenance call soon
- Who hasn't been contacted in 6 months
- Where your best customers live
- Which jobs make the most money

Use this to call people and book more work.

---
