Paid search should match the job and the service area

Google Ads for contractors works best when a campaign mirrors the way the company operates. Emergency calls, planned estimates, residential work, and commercial bids need different keywords, messages, landing pages, and follow-up. Combining them hides which part of the spend is useful.

Before launch, we confirm the jobs to promote, areas to exclude, hours someone can answer, and what counts as a qualified lead. Search terms are reviewed for irrelevant intent, and the landing page is treated as part of the campaign. Contractor website design and past-customer reactivation may solve different gaps without forcing every goal into paid search.

What belongs in a contractor ad account?

  • Separate campaigns or groups for services with different value and intent.
  • Location, schedule, device, and exclusion settings that match operations.
  • Call, form, and landing-page tracking with lead-quality review.
  • A change log and reporting that separates spend, inquiries, and outcomes.

Google Ads for contractors with accountable spend. FAQ

How much should a contractor spend on Google Ads?

A useful budget depends on service value, search demand, market competition, service area, close rate, capacity, and the quality of the landing page. Toughjobs reviews those inputs before recommending a range. Media spend and management fees should be shown separately so the owner can compare the full cost.

How quickly can a paid campaign start?

Launch follows account access, tracking, service and location decisions, landing-page readiness, ad approval, and a way to answer leads. Speed is less important than a clean setup. A rushed campaign without exclusions or working call tracking can spend money before the business has reliable evidence.

Do you use broad match keywords?

Match types are tools, not a universal rule. The right choice depends on conversion data, search volume, query quality, negatives, budget, and how tightly the service can be described. Toughjobs reviews the actual search terms and lead quality instead of assuming one match type is always safe.

How are phone leads tracked?

Call tracking can connect a call with the campaign and landing page while preserving the company’s main business number where appropriate. The setup, recording policy, consent requirements, routing, and retention need to match the business and applicable law. Form leads should be tracked through the same reporting path.
